Ask about a Tally

Your teams, live, on the wall.

Tally is an LED scoreboard for your home. It shows live scores for the teams you follow, celebrates when they score, and fills the time between games with weather, scenes and what's on TV.

Ask about getting one

What it does

Set it up once from your phone. After that it just runs.

Live scores

Pro and college football, basketball, baseball, hockey and soccer, plus golf, F1, NASCAR, IndyCar, UFC and tennis. Follow your teams, whole leagues or conferences.

Big-moment alerts

Touchdowns, field goals, home runs, goals, upsets and wins get their own animation the moment they happen.

Between the games

Dozens of animated scenes that follow the time of day and the seasons, from a campfire to the northern lights, with the clock in the corner.

Weather

Today's forecast, rain on the way, and the weather at the stadium for outdoor games.

Movies and TV

What's in theaters, what's new to stream, and when your shows' next episodes and seasons arrive.

A remote in your pocket

Skip ahead, watch one game, dim it or put it to sleep from any phone on your Wi-Fi. No app to install.

Night hours

It dims or sleeps when you want it to, and wakes up with a sunrise.

Keeps itself up to date

New features and fixes arrive over Wi-Fi by themselves.

On the panel

Frames drawn by Tally's own display code.

Setting it up

About five minutes, all from your phone.

  1. Plug it inTally starts up and opens its own Wi-Fi network for setup.
  2. Connect it to your Wi-FiJoin its network from your phone and pick your home Wi-Fi.
  3. Pick your teamsChoose your teams, your town for the weather, and a look. That's it.

Questions

Is there a subscription?

No. There's no account and nothing to pay after you have it.

Do I need an app?

No. You set it up and change it from your phone's or computer's web browser, on your home Wi-Fi.

What Wi-Fi does it need?

A normal home Wi-Fi network on 2.4 GHz, which almost every home router provides.

Can it follow more than one team?

Yes, as many as you like, across sports. It shows the ones playing now first.

What about my privacy?

Your settings stay on the board itself. Tally doesn't need an account, and it only reaches out for scores, weather and updates.

What if something goes wrong?

Its Health page can send us a note with what it's seeing, or email tallyboards@gmail.com.
