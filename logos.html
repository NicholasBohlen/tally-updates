How Tally uses logos

Last updated November 16, 2026

Tally is a scoreboard: it shows games, scores and schedules for the teams you follow. To make a game easy to recognize at a glance across a room, it can show each team's logo next to its name and score, the way a newspaper's sports page or a TV score bug does.

Who owns the logos

Every team, league, conference, school, airline, racing team and tournament logo shown by Tally is the property of its owner. They're used only to identify the team or event a score or schedule is about, and only as much as that needs.

No affiliation or endorsement

Tally isn't affiliated with, sponsored by, licensed by or endorsed by any team, league, conference, school, governing body, broadcaster or company whose name or logo appears on it. Showing a logo doesn't mean its owner approves of or works with Tally. Tally is not an official product of any of them.

Asking us to take a logo down

If you own a logo or act for its owner and don't want it shown by Tally, email tallyboards@gmail.com with:

  • the logo and the team, league or organization it belongs to,
  • who you are and your connection to the owner, and
  • how we can reach you.

We'll reply promptly. Once we take a logo down, every Tally removes it the next time it checks in (normally within a day), and shows a plain badge with the team's abbreviation and colors in its place. We can remove any logo at any time, for any reason.

Logos owners add themselves

A Tally owner can add a picture of their own for a team on their own board. Those pictures stay on that owner's board for their personal use; we don't receive, check or share them. Owners are responsible for having the right to use what they add.
